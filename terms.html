1. Services Overview

Hyrzilla LLC provides two categories of recruitment services:

  • Talent Solutions: Active sourcing of job seekers, resume building, job applications, interview coordination, and placement support — governed by individual service agreements.
  • Corporate Solutions: Introduction of vetted job seekers to companies with open positions — governed by corporate placement agreements.

⚠️ Best-Effort Service: Hyrzilla provides strategic career and placement services. While our processes are designed to maximize placement success, ultimate hiring decisions rest with the employer. Placement is dependent on market conditions and individual qualifications.

2. Candidate Enrollment Terms

2.1 Enrollment & Pricing

To begin Hyrzilla's candidate services, you must sign an individual Service Agreement which will outline your chosen plan (Base, Growth, or Pro) and the specific upfront and success-based fees associated with it. Services commence only after this agreement is signed and any initial fees are processed.

2.2 Refund Policy

Details regarding refunds for any upfront fees are strictly governed by your individual Service Agreement. Generally, fees are refundable within a 7-day window provided that no active work (such as resume rewriting or coaching) has commenced.

2.3 Success Fee

Upon acceptance of a job offer sourced or facilitated through Hyrzilla, you agree to pay a success fee calculated as a percentage of your accepted annual salary, as detailed in your Service Agreement. This fee is due within 30 days of your first day of employment.

2.4 Pass-Through Fees

Some employers require pre-employment screening, including background checks and drug tests. If required, these fees are billed to you at exact cost — Hyrzilla does not profit from them. Hyrzilla will always notify you before such fees are incurred and will require your explicit authorization.

2.5 Candidate Obligations

To enable effective service delivery, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate and complete information about your experience, skills, and employment history
  • Notify Hyrzilla promptly if you receive, accept, or decline any job offers (whether sourced by Hyrzilla or otherwise)
  • Cooperate with interview scheduling and feedback requests

3. Corporate (Employer) Terms

3.1 Agreement Requirement

Before Hyrzilla shares any candidate resume or detailed profile, the company must sign a Corporate Placement Agreement. This agreement specifies the success fee structure and the candidates covered.

3.2 Placement Fee

Corporate clients are not charged an upfront retainer. Hyrzilla operates on a contingency basis. The company agrees to pay a placement fee (a percentage of the candidate's first-year annualized compensation) only if a presented candidate is hired. The fee is due upon the candidate's start date, net 30 terms.

3.3 Candidate Ownership

If a company hires a candidate presented by Hyrzilla for any role within 12 months of the initial presentation, the placement fee is owed, regardless of whether the hire was for the initially discussed role.
