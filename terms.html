1. Services Overview

Hyrzilla LLC provides two categories of recruitment services:

  • Candidate Enrollment Services (Bench Sales): Active sourcing of job seekers, resume building, job applications, interview coordination, and placement support — in exchange for an upfront enrollment fee plus a success percentage.
  • Company Placement Services: Introduction of vetted job seekers to companies with open positions — in exchange for a success fee paid on the candidate's first day of employment.

⚠️ No Job Guarantee: Hyrzilla does not guarantee employment or job offers. We provide best-effort recruitment services. Placement depends on market conditions, employer decisions, and candidate qualifications — all of which are outside Hyrzilla's control.

2. Candidate Enrollment Terms

2.1 Enrollment Fee

To begin Hyrzilla's candidate services, you must pay an upfront enrollment fee corresponding to your chosen plan:

  • Base Plan: $499 upfront
  • Growth Plan: $1,499 upfront
  • Pro Plan: $2,499 upfront

This fee covers the cost of resume rewriting, LinkedIn optimization (where applicable), job applications, and support as defined by your plan. It is charged at the time of enrollment and is required before any work begins.

2.2 Refund Policy

The enrollment fee is refundable within 7 calendar days of enrollment, provided that no work has commenced (i.e., the resume rewrite has not been initiated). After work has begun or after 7 days (whichever comes first), the enrollment fee is non-refundable.

2.3 Success Fee

Upon acceptance of a job offer sourced or facilitated through Hyrzilla, you agree to pay a success fee calculated as a percentage of your accepted annual salary:

  • Base Plan: 15% of annual salary
  • Growth Plan: 12% of annual salary
  • Pro Plan: 10% of annual salary

The success fee is due within 30 days of your first day of employment and is paid directly to Hyrzilla via the agreed payment method. Failure to remit the success fee within this period may result in late fees and/or legal action.

2.4 Pass-Through Fees

Some employers require pre-employment screening, including background checks (~$30–$80) and drug tests (~$30–$60). These fees are billed to you at exact cost — Hyrzilla does not profit from them. Hyrzilla will always notify you before such fees are incurred and will require your explicit authorization.

2.5 Plan Upgrades

You may upgrade from a lower plan to a higher plan at any time. You will be charged the difference in upfront fees. The success percentage of the new plan applies from the date of the upgrade.

2.6 Candidate Obligations

To enable effective service delivery, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate and complete information about your experience, skills, and employment history
  • Notify Hyrzilla promptly if you receive, accept, or decline any job offers (whether sourced by Hyrzilla or otherwise)
  • Not independently apply to positions that Hyrzilla has already submitted you to, during the active enrollment period
  • Cooperate with interview scheduling and feedback requests

3.1 Agreement Requirement

Before Hyrzilla shares any candidate resume or detailed profile, the company must sign a Placement Agreement. This agreement specifies:

  • The success fee percentage (10–15% of annual package, as agreed)

3.2 Success Fee — Companies

If a company hires a candidate introduced by Hyrzilla, the company agrees to pay the success fee stated in the Placement Agreement on or before the candidate's first day of employment. This fee is due regardless of whether the candidate was the primary or backup candidate.

3.3 Replacement Clause

If a placed candidate leaves within 60 days of starting for reasons within Hyrzilla's control, Hyrzilla will make reasonable efforts to source a replacement at no additional placement fee. This clause applies only once per placement.

3.4 Non-Circumvention

Companies agree not to hire any candidate introduced by Hyrzilla while bypassing Hyrzilla's process to avoid the placement fee, for a period of 12 months from the date of introduction.
