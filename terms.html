Terms & Conditions

Effective Date: January 1, 2024 · Last Updated: November 2024

These Terms and Conditions ("Terms") govern your use of Hyrzilla LLC's advisory and staffing services. By engaging with Hyrzilla, you agree to be bound by these Terms. Please review them carefully.

1. Scope of Services

Hyrzilla LLC operates as a strategic talent advisory and enterprise staffing firm, providing two primary service categories:

  • Career Advisory: Technical profile architecture, market representation, interview strategy, and executive placement support for engineering professionals.
  • Enterprise Staffing: Sourcing, vetting, and contingency placement of elite technical talent for corporate partners.

⚠️ Consultative Best-Effort Delivery: Hyrzilla provides strategic advisory services designed to maximize market impact. However, ultimate hiring and compensation decisions reside exclusively with the employer. Hyrzilla cannot ethically guarantee employment outcomes.

2. Career Advisory Agreements (Talent)

2.1 Retainer Fees

To initiate a Career Advisory engagement, professionals must submit an upfront retainer corresponding to their selected tier:

  • Market Readiness Plan: $499 upfront
  • Strategic Acceleration Plan: $1,499 upfront
  • Executive Partnership Plan: $2,499 upfront

This retainer secures our advisory bandwidth, encompassing technical narrative architecture, market research, and dedicated advocacy. Work commences immediately upon execution of the agreement.

2.2 Refund Parameters

The upfront retainer is fully refundable within 7 calendar days of execution, provided that no advisory work (e.g., resume architecture, market deployment) has commenced. Once strategic execution begins, or after 7 days elapse, the retainer is strictly non-refundable.

2.3 Success Fee Structure

Upon acceptance of an offer facilitated by Hyrzilla's advocacy, the professional agrees to remit a placement success fee based on their accepted annual base salary:

  • Market Readiness Plan: 15% of annual salary
  • Strategic Acceleration Plan: 12% of annual salary
  • Executive Partnership Plan: 10% of annual salary

Success fees are payable within 30 days of the professional’s start date. Failure to remit fees within this window may result in contractual penalties or legal remediation.

2.4 Independent Sourcing

Should the professional secure a role entirely independent of Hyrzilla's network and advocacy efforts, success fees may be waived or adjusted. Explicit parameters governing independent sourcing are detailed within the formal signed Career Advisory Agreement.

3. Enterprise Staffing Agreements (Corporate)

3.1 Contingency Execution

Enterprise clients are not subject to upfront retainers. Hyrzilla operates on a strict contingency basis. Fees are only realized upon the successful hiring and integration of a candidate presented by our firm.

3.2 Placement Fees

The enterprise agrees to remit a placement fee (calculated as a percentage of the candidate's first-year annualized compensation) upon the candidate's start date. Standard payment terms are Net 30, unless otherwise specified in the customized Corporate Placement Agreement.

3.3 Candidate Representation Rights

If an enterprise hires a candidate introduced by Hyrzilla for any role within twelve (12) months of the initial presentation, the placement fee is owed in full, regardless of whether the hire aligns with the originally discussed requisition.

4. Confidentiality & Discretion

Hyrzilla operates with absolute discretion. We do not disclose candidate profiles, enterprise architectures, or strategic roadmaps to unauthorized third parties. Both talent and enterprise partners are expected to maintain the confidentiality of any proprietary intelligence shared during the engagement.
