Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2024 · Last Updated: November 2024

1. Who We Are

Hyrzilla LLC ("Hyrzilla", "we", "our", or "us") is a US-based IT recruitment firm. We operate at hyrzilla.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Identity data: First name, last name, professional title
  • Contact data: Email address, phone number, WhatsApp number
  • Professional data: Resume, work history, skills, LinkedIn profile URL, target job preferences
  • Financial data: Plan selection and payment records (payment processing handled by third-party providers; we do not store card numbers)
  • Usage data: Pages visited, time on site, referral source (via cookies and analytics tools)
  • Communications: Emails, form submissions, and messages you send us

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information for the following purposes:

  • To provide recruitment services (resume building, job applications, interview scheduling)
  • To match job seekers with company opportunities
  • To communicate with you about your enrollment, applications, and offers
  • To process payments for enrollment plans and success fees
  • To coordinate third-party services you authorize (background checks, drug tests)
  • To send service-related updates (not marketing emails without consent)
  • To improve our website and services through analytics

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data with:

  • Companies (Employers): Only after you enroll and only with your explicit understanding that your resume will be shared as part of the service
  • Third-party vendors: Background check providers and drug test facilities (only when authorized by you and required by the hiring process)
  • Payment processors: Secure third-party processors (e.g., Stripe) for plan fees and success fee collection
  • Analytics providers: Google Analytics or equivalent tools (anonymized, aggregated data only)
  • Legal authorities: When required by law or to protect our legal rights

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes above. For enrolled candidates, we retain records for a minimum of 3 years after placement to comply with legal and contractual obligations. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 7).

6. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies (for functionality) and analytics cookies (to understand traffic). You can disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to or restrict how we process your data
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@hyrzilla.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, access controls, and secure data storage. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and not share your account credentials.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated via email or a notice on our website. Continued use of our services after updates constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, reach us at:
Email: privacy@hyrzilla.com
Website: hyrzilla.com/contact
