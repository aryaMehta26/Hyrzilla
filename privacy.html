Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Last Updated: November 2026

Hyrzilla LLC ("Hyrzilla," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of the professionals and enterprises we advise. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, utilize, and secure your information.

1. Information We Collect

In the execution of our advisory and staffing operations, we collect specific data points to align technical talent with enterprise requirements:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Name, email address, phone number, LinkedIn profiles, and geographical location.
  • Professional Data: Resumes, technical portfolios, employment history, compensation expectations, and interview performance metrics.
  • Enterprise Data: Corporate roadmaps, technical stack details, internal team structures, and hiring requisitions.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser types, and usage telemetry collected via our digital platform.

2. Utilization of Data

We process your data exclusively to facilitate strategic placements and improve our advisory services:

  • To architect and optimize professional profiles for market deployment.
  • To present vetted candidates to enterprise partners (strictly upon candidate consent).
  • To process retainer payments and calculate contingency success fees.
  • To analyze market trends and calibrate our internal intelligence frameworks.

3. Disclosure of Information

Hyrzilla operates with absolute discretion. We do not sell your personal data. We disclose information only under the following parameters:

  • To Enterprise Partners: We share professional profiles with hiring organizations only after conducting strategic alignment and receiving explicit authorization from the candidate.
  • To Operational Vendors: We may share data with secure third-party infrastructure providers (e.g., secure cloud hosting, payment gateways) required to run our operations.
  • Legal Imperatives: If mandated by law or court order, we will disclose necessary information to relevant authorities.

4. Data Security & Retention

We implement robust cryptographic and operational security measures to prevent unauthorized access to your data. Data is retained only for the duration necessary to fulfill the objectives outlined in this policy or as required by corporate compliance standards.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you hold specific rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access & Portability: Request a secure export of the data we hold on you.
  • Correction: Request immediate rectification of inaccurate or outdated professional records.
  • Erasure: Request the deletion of your data from our active placement frameworks (subject to legal or contractual retention requirements).

6. Contact the Data Officer

For inquiries regarding our data operations or to exercise your privacy rights, please contact our internal compliance team:
