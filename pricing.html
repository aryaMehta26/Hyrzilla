FAQ

Pricing questions,
answered honestly.

The success fee (10–15% depending on your plan) is paid from your first month's salary, after you have accepted the job offer and started working. It is not due before you start.
The upfront fee is refundable within 7 days of enrollment if no work has started (i.e., resume rewrite has not been initiated). After work begins, the fee is non-refundable. See full terms in our Terms & Conditions.
No. Hyrzilla does not guarantee a job offer. We guarantee our full effort — resume optimization, active applications, interview prep, and coordination. Placement depends on your profile, the market, and employer decisions, which are outside our control.
If you do not receive a job offer during the active engagement period, you do not pay the success fee. The upfront plan fee is non-refundable (it covers the work already done — resume, applications, etc.). We will discuss next steps and options with you honestly.
Some employers require a background check (~$30–80) and/or drug test (~$30–60) before finalizing an offer. These are pass-through costs — we collect the exact amount from you and pay the third-party vendor. We do not mark these up.
Yes. You can upgrade from Base to Growth, or Growth to Pro at any time. You pay only the difference in the upfront fee. The success percentage adjusts to your new plan from the point of upgrade.
Companies pay 10–15% of the hired candidate's annual salary package on Day 1 of their employment. The exact percentage is stated in the signed agreement before any candidate resume is shared. There is no other cost to the company.
