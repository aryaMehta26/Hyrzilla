Corporate Partners

Hire without the upfront risk.

We do not charge upfront retainer fees for companies. We operate on a contingency basis, meaning you only pay a placement fee when the candidate we provide starts their first day of work.

Request Placement Agreement

How the partnership works:

  • 1. Discovery Call: We discuss your technical requirements, team culture, and budget.
  • 2. Agreement Signed: We send our standard placement agreement outlining the success fee structure.
  • 3. Candidate Review: You receive pre-vetted resumes matching your exact criteria.
  • 4. Hire & Pay: You hire the best fit and pay the success fee on their start date.
