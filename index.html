The Process

Every step.
Handled by us.

We work the entire recruitment funnel — from finding you on a portal to sitting in the interview lobby with you (figuratively). Here's exactly what happens after you enroll.

View Plans & Pricing →
01

Discovery

We find you on job portals

Our team scans LinkedIn, Indeed, Dice, and other US job platforms for active IT professionals. We reach out with an honest introduction — no pressure.

02

Enrollment

Enrollment call & plan selection

We learn your background, target role, salary expectations, and work preferences. You choose a plan (Base / Growth / Pro) based on how much support you need.

03

Build

Resume, LinkedIn & application strategy

We rewrite your resume for ATS systems, optimize your LinkedIn, and build a target company list aligned to your goals.

04

Apply

Active job applications every week

We apply to positions on your behalf — cover letters, portals, direct outreach to hiring managers. 20–unlimited applications per month depending on your plan.

05

Placement

Interviews, offer, and settlement

We schedule and coordinate interviews. Once you receive and accept a job offer, the success fee (10–15% of annual salary, paid from month 1) is settled. That's it.
