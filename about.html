Our Story

Built by recruiters who got
burned by bad hires.

We saw how slow, opaque, and transactional IT hiring had become. So we built something that actually works.

Mission

We make IT hiring
human again.

Tech hiring is broken. Recruiters blast candidates to dozens of companies. Companies wait 6 weeks for a shortlist that doesn't fit. Everyone wastes time.

Hyrzilla operates on a different model — deep specificity over volume. Every search is calibrated to the role, the team, the culture, and the individual. The result: fewer candidates submitted, better fits, faster placements.

Our Journey

From startup
to national firm.

2016

Founded in New York City

Two ex-recruiters with one goal: make IT hiring honest. Closed 12 placements in month one.

2018

Expanded to 8 US Markets

Austin, Chicago, San Francisco, Seattle. 500+ placements crossed.

2020

Remote-First Pivot

Built a fully virtual vetting process during the pandemic — now an industry benchmark.

2022

AI & ML Practice Launched

Dedicated division for AI, ML, and Data Engineering — now our fastest-growing segment.

2025

Rebranded to Hyrzilla

1,820+ placements. 320+ partners. A new name for a sharpened mission.
