What We Do

Two services.
One mission.

We serve job seekers who need a recruitment partner, and companies who need the right hire — without upfront risk.

For Job Seekers

Enroll. We handle the rest.
You focus on the interview.

You pay an upfront plan fee to start. Then, when you land and accept a job offer, you pay a success percentage from your first month's salary. There is no job guarantee — we give our full effort, every time.

📄

Resume Rewrite

ATS-optimised resume written by our recruiters who know exactly what US hiring managers look for.

💼

LinkedIn Optimization

Profile headline, summary, and experience rewritten to attract recruiter searches and inbound messages.

🚀

Active Job Applications

We apply on your behalf — 20 to unlimited per month based on your plan. Tailored cover letters, direct portal submissions.

🎤

Interview Preparation

Role-specific prep, common questions, and feedback coaching. Available on Growth and Pro plans.

🎯

Mock Interviews

Live mock sessions with real recruiter feedback. Pro plan only — includes technical and behavioral rounds.

💰

Salary Negotiation Coaching

We coach your strategy and can advocate directly with the employer on counter-offers. Pro plan only.

For Companies

We bring candidates to you.
You pay only when you hire.

No upfront cost. No subscriptions. Just a straightforward agreement and a success fee when a candidate starts work.

🔍

Proactive Candidate Sourcing

We identify job seekers from portals, vet them against your open roles, and reach out to you with matching profiles.

📑

Agreement First

Before sharing any candidate resume, we send a simple agreement outlining the success fee percentage. Signed digitally, takes 5 minutes.

✅

Pay on Day One

A candidate is placed → they start work → you pay 10–15% of their annual package on their first day of employment. Not before.

How the Agreement Works

Step 1
We reach out

We contact your hiring team with a brief intro and a matching candidate profile overview (no resume yet).

Step 2
Sign agreement

You sign a one-page agreement stating the success fee (10–15% based on role type). Only then do we share the full resume.

Step 3
Hire & pay

You interview, extend an offer, they accept, they start. On Day 1 of employment, you remit the agreed success fee. No exceptions.
