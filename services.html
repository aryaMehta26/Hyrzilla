What We Do

Tailored IT placement & recruitment solutions.

We provide dedicated support for IT talent looking for career growth, alongside fast, reliable contingency staffing for companies seeking skilled developers.

Career Advisory & Talent Services

Finding the right tech role takes more than just sending resumes. We guide you through resume optimization, targeted job outreach, interview coaching, and salary negotiation.

1. Resume & Profile Rebuilding

We redesign your resume and LinkedIn profile to highlight your technical projects, tech stack, and key metrics so hiring managers notice you instantly.

2. Job Portal & Direct Application Support

We systematically identify relevant openings across job portals and company career pages, applying on your behalf to save you time and maximize callbacks.

3. Mock Interviews & Prep

Practice with mock technical and behavioral interviews. Get real feedback so you can clear screening calls and technical rounds without stress.

4. Offer Guidance & Negotiation

Once you receive a job offer, we help you evaluate compensation terms and assist with salary negotiation to get you the package you deserve.
